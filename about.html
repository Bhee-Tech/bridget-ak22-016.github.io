About me

A little about Bridget

I am studying Computer Science, matric number AK22/PHS/CSC/016. I enjoy the part of the course where you can see the result of what you typed appear on the screen straight away, which is most of what web development is.

How I built this

Everything on both websites was typed by hand in Notepad. I did not use a website builder, a template, or a framework like Bootstrap. The reason is simple: if I did not type it, I could not explain it.

It took longer this way. But I understand every line, and that was the point of the assignment.

What I learned

The biggest lesson came from the bookshop. A shopping cart has to remember what you added, and the page has to redraw itself every time the cart changes. Working out where to keep that information was the hardest part, and the most useful.

What I used

Three tools, nothing else

STRUCTURE

HTML

Every page, every heading, every book on the shelf. HTML decides what is on the page and in what order.

APPEARANCE

CSS

One stylesheet, shared by all five pages and the bookshop. It sets the colours, the fonts, the spacing and the layout.

BEHAVIOUR

JavaScript

Only used in the bookshop, and only for the cart. Everything else on the site works without any JavaScript at all.
