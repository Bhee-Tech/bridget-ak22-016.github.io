The hard part

How the cart remembers things

When you add a book, the shop has to remember it. My first idea was to read the numbers off the screen whenever I needed them, but that quickly became confusing, because the screen and the real total kept disagreeing.

The answer was to keep one list in JavaScript, called cart, and treat it as the only truth. Nothing is ever read off the screen. When the list changes, I wipe the cart area and draw it again from the list. The screen is always just a picture of the list.
